# ComPILAR Draft model, in development (provisional)

Draft model built by scripts/draft/build_pillars.py from canon/pillars.json (V1, frozen) and docs/model-edits/draft-set-2026-10-30.md. PROVISIONAL: Ben approved the set and four rulings (30 Oct 2026, 11.50 am to 12.40 pm) but not the Pillar texts.

- Egalitarian deck: GrpProsp, Reciprocity, Belonging, Levelling, DivsExp, OutResp
- Hierarchical deck: OwnProsp, Reciprocity, Belonging, Status, NormExp, Prestige

## Pillars

### GrpProsp: Group Prospects

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: ECP. Axis: Prospects

How far you feel the group, as it now stands, can pull off the venture it has agreed to take on and not yet finished: a game, project, season or campaign. It is confidence in the group's capability for that venture, its members' skill included: not luck, not your own prospects (OwnProsp), not whether each will do their part (OutResp), not a general sense that the group is strong.

- 10: You feel sure the group, as it stands, can pull off what it has set itself; the venture looks well within its reach.
- 1: You feel sure the group, as it stands, cannot pull off what it has set itself; the venture looks beyond it.
- Pre-language: A felt sense, before any words, that this band can bring off what it is setting out to do: the chimpanzee weighing the hunting party and feeling the colobus within reach when enough able hunters are present, and out of reach when they are not. Intimates read it from her bearing as the venture starts: settled and forward-leaning, in step with the others, or hesitant and watchful, eyes on the others rather than the task.

Effect on Engagement: That is likely to succeed in goals that you believe are worth pursing, or you would not have joined the group in the first place, and that has agreed goals, or otherwise random changes to the group's direction will move outside the model's ability to predict.

### OwnProsp: Own Prospects

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: HCP. Axis: Prospects

How likely you feel it is that staying in this group will get you what you yourself are after from it: the place, play, pay, skill, advancement or mate you joined for. It is a forecast of what the group will deliver to you, not your own ability, not the group's own venture, and not a tally of what it has given so far, which only feeds it.

- 10: You are sure that staying will bring you what you came for; the group's dealings with you keep opening the way to it.
- 1: You are sure that staying will bring you nothing you came for; whatever you do here, the way to it stays shut.
- Pre-language: The felt yield of staying with this band for her: whether being here keeps bringing her food, a mate, a place or a skill, or keeps bringing her nothing. Its animal form is the forager's reading that a patch is still worth working, and the primate's reading that staying in this troop will or will not yield what staying costs. Intimates read it from whether she settles in and works at her own aims here, or drifts to the edge, looks outward and leaves.

Effect on Engagement: Where you feel that you are likely to achieve your personal goals, whether or not they are part of the group goals (GrpProsp) or indeed, conflict with them. Presumably if personal and group goals are in conflict, you would prefer to have low levels of GrpProsp however such a condition is out of scope for the model as it indicates deception and potentially sabotage, which is a cognitive rather than an unconscious drive (as the Pillars are contended to be).

### Status: Status

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: HCP. Axis: Standing

Your felt rung in the group's settled order of precedence: how far you go first, take the first turn and the first share as a matter of course, and how far you go after others and take what is left. All recognise the order, so it is not fought out each time. The precedence attaches to your place, however you came by it: an office, seniority, the backing of kin or allies, an old contest no one reopens, or credit for what you bring that has settled, over time, into a place others take for granted. It is distinct from the live deference you draw for what you bring now (Prestige), and it is not held in place by present fear (Levelling).

- 10: You go first as a matter of course: the first turn, the first share and the best seat are yours without a word said, and no one in the group stands above you.
- 1: You go last as a matter of course: you wait your turn and take your share and seat after everyone else, without a word said, and no one in the group stands below you.
- Pre-language: Her felt rung in an order all recognise: the others let her pass, eat and settle first and greet her with the calm signals of submission, or she passes, eats and settles after them and greets them so, and neither side has to fight it out each time. Intimates read it from who passes first when paths cross, who eats first, who takes the best spot, and who greets whom. Read with care: beliefs about the bodily signs of rank outrun the measured signs.

Effect on Engagement: Where you feel that you possess formal power within a group, enabling you to utilise the group to achieve your own vision, which aligns the group with your identity. This is why new leaders often revoke measures taken by the preceding.

### NormExp: Normative Expression (norm guarding)

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: HCP. Axis: Expression

How strongly it strikes you as wrong when another member breaks from the group's settled ways, in act or by openly pressing to change them. It is felt indignation at a breach, not dislike of the person. Intimates read it from whether you pull the member up: an objection, mockery, a cold shoulder or shutting them out. Your own conformity and your openness to ideas are not part of it.

- 10: The smallest departure jars you at once as plainly wrong, and intimates see you pull the member up on the spot: a sharp word, a mocking laugh or a turned back.
- 1: A member doing or urging something different leaves you unmoved; nothing in you says it is wrong, and intimates see you let it pass.
- Pre-language: The bristle at one of the band doing a thing the wrong way. Its sign is the move to pull them up: a protest cry, a shove, a cold shoulder or exclusion. Two and three year olds already protest a puppet's wrong move (Rakoczy et al. 2008); Ju/'hoansi pull up breaches by criticism and mockery (Wiessner 2005); chimpanzees retaliate for their own losses but do not police third-party breaches (Riedl et al. 2012). Intimates read it from how she meets another's departure: with indignation and a rebuke, or with a shrug. Forager bands show the same bristle turned on upstarts and stingy members; in this model that egalitarian form is carried by DivsExp->Levelling. The face here is the guarding of a ranked group's settled ways.

Effect on Engagement: Where you feel that you can manage (minimise unwelcome or surprising) change, by using your power to reduce any propensity for others to suggest change. While high NormExp undermines Psychological Safety for others, it makes you personally feel safer.

### OutResp: Outgoing Respect

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: ECP. Axis: Respect

How far you feel the other members can be counted on to do their part in the shared work: each carries through what they take on and keeps their word, so you leave work in their hands without checking. It is reliability, not skill (GrpProsp): a willing but clumsy member rates high. It reads their part, not the return on your help (Reciprocity), nor their trust in you.

- 10: You leave any part of the work with any member and never check up on it; what they take on gets done and what they say they will do holds.
- 1: You check up on, chase and redo others' work, expecting them to shirk, cut corners or go back on their word.
- Pre-language: Whether she can turn her back on them at a shared task. One who counts on her fellows leaves the watch, the carry or the young in their hands and goes about her own business without looking back; one who does not keeps glancing over, hovers at their work or takes it over herself. Intimates read it from how often she looks back, checks and takes over. The mechanism, trust calibrated to a partner's record, is the one chimpanzees show when they adjust trust to partners who have come through before (Engelmann et al. 2015); what sets this Pillar apart from Reciprocity is what the record is of: the partner's share of a joint task, not a favour returned to her. It is her reading of them; whether they turn their backs on her is a different thing and not read here.

Effect on Engagement: With competent and trustworthy people because you like being associated with 'winners', you admire and wish to model yourself off them, and they can help you to improve.

### Levelling: Levelling

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: ECP. Axis: Standing

How far you feel no one in the group sets themselves over you: makes you do, drop or endure things by force or fear, or takes more than the group's own reckoning gives them (of shares, credit or say) and makes it stick. Your part rests on going along, not on being made afraid or being shortchanged. It is not rank (Status), acceptance (Belonging), deference for what you bring (Prestige), the return on help (Reciprocity) or the cost of showing yourself (DivsExp). A respected leader, or a settled order you accept, leaves it high; a member you must appease, or one who grabs and gets away with it, lowers it.

- 10: No one in the group can make you do or drop anything by force or threat, and no one takes more than their due and keeps it. You move, take your share and go about your business in the strongest member's presence as freely as in anyone's.
- 1: One or a few members make you comply through force or fear, or take more than their due and keep it. You watch them warily, fall quiet when they come near, and let them have what they want to head off their anger.
- Pre-language: Freedom from a bully she must appease. The free animal moves, feeds and takes her share in the strongest member's presence without flinching or a glance at that member's mood; the coerced one tracks that member's moods, freezes on approach and does whatever heads off an attack. Intimates read it from whether she watches one particular member warily and shrinks or placates when that member comes near, while staying easy with everyone else.

### DivsExp: Diverse Expression (felt safety)

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: ECP. Axis: Expression

How safe you feel to show yourself in this group: to let a slip, a mood, a need or a disagreement show, expecting no attack, mockery or rebuff for it. It is your reading of what showing yourself will cost you, not whether you speak up, whether your word carries weight (Prestige), whether you are wanted (Belonging) or whether anyone pushes you about by force or fear (Levelling).

- 10: You let slips, moods, needs and disagreements show as they come, expecting no one to pounce on them, laugh at them or turn away.
- 1: You scan faces before you move, keep your reactions masked and hold back even a plain need or disagreement, braced for a put-down.
- Pre-language: Relaxed against braced among her own: the loose body and open play of an animal that expects no bite from its fellows, against the scanning, self-scratching stillness of one that does (displacement activity rises with social stress: Maestripieri et al. 1992). Intimates read it from how loose or tight she is when eyes turn to her, and from whether she hides a hurt or a want.

Effect on Engagement: Where you feel that your ideas will be welcomed and well-received. While related to IncResp (HCP), Expression is closer to a norm of Psychological Safety than being given specific respect for one's competence. Such a norm creates a warm, less hostile environment, which is distinct from cohesion: a close, friendly group can still make challenge feel risky.

### Reciprocity: Reciprocity

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: shared. Axis: Reciprocity

Your felt reading of whether help in this group comes back: what you give is returned, what you ask for is met. It holds until evidence moves it. It concerns the return on help, not whether others carry their part of shared work (OutResp), whether anyone takes more of a common take than their due (Levelling) or where you stand (Status). The egalitarian face reads the return as coming from the group; the hierarchical face, from the partner helped.

- 10: Help goes where it is needed and comes back as a matter of course; you count on the return without a second thought, because it has never failed.
- 1: What you give is not returned and what you need goes unmet; you expect nothing back from anyone here.
- Pre-language: The felt return on sharing: grooming that is returned by those groomed (Schino and Aureli 2008) and forager food handed over on demand that comes back from the band's next kill (Peterson 1993). Intimates read it as a sign in her behaviour: whether she hands over on request and asks as of right, expecting the return, or hesitates, hoards and goes without rather than ask.

Face in the egalitarian deck: Return from the group. Help goes to whoever needs it, and the return comes from the group, not from the one helped. It is conditional on membership and standing, not on a tally: a known sharer is given to, a known hoarder is passed over, and whoever has a surplus is pressed to share on demand. Your reading is whether the group pays back what you put into it.
- 10: Whoever has gives to whoever needs; what you have put in comes back from any member, and you are pressed to share as one of us.
- 1: Giving goes into a hole: nothing comes back from anyone, those with a surplus hide it, and need goes unmet.
- Pre-language: Food handed over on demand within the band, with the return coming from whoever next has a surplus; those who hide food lose standing and are passed over. Intimates read it as a sign in her behaviour: whether she hands her surplus to anyone who asks and asks anyone who has, or holds back from both.

Face in the hierarchical deck: Return from the partner. Help flows between particular partners who return it to each other. It is conditional on a return from the same partner: in kind between equals, or across rank, as favour and protection passed down for loyalty and service passed up. Your reading is whether your partners pay you back, not where you stand (Status) or whether they do their part of the shared work (OutResp).
- 10: Every partner you help pays you back and those you ask deliver; your patron covers you and your people back you, each in turn.
- 1: Partners take and do not return; no one above covers you and no one below backs you; you expect no return from anyone you deal with.
- Pre-language: Grooming returned by the one groomed and support in fights traded between the same two (Schino and Aureli 2008; Schino 2007), with asymmetric exchange across rank: tolerance and protection down, support and deference up. Intimates read it as a sign in her behaviour: whether she keeps giving to those who gave to her and turns from those who did not.

### Belonging: Belonging

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: shared. Axis: Belonging

How far you feel the others want you among them: whether they make room for you and draw you in, or leave you at the edge. It is their acceptance of you, read from how they treat you, not whether they defer to you for skill or contribution (Prestige) or where you stand in the order (Status). The culture's face sets what the acceptance rests on.

- 10: Others make room for you on sight and draw you into whatever is going on; you never doubt that you are wanted here.
- 1: Gatherings form without you and you are left at the edge; you doubt that anyone wants you here.
- Pre-language: Being let into the huddle or kept out of it, time after time. The others shift to make a place, share, groom and turn towards her, or close ranks, turn away and leave her at the fringe, and keep doing so. Intimates read it from whether she moves freely into the middle of the group or hovers at its edge, watching for a gap.

Face in the egalitarian deck: Wanted as one of their own in good standing. The others want you among them as one of their own in good standing: you pull with the group and are no burden on it. The acceptance does not rise or fall with your output or with any place in an order, but it is not unconditional: a member who shirks, cheats or feeds on the others is edged out. You read liking from the group as a whole.
- 10: Others make room for you and draw you in as a matter of course, and have done so through good seasons and bad; nothing short of your turning against the group would change it.
- 1: Gatherings form without you and you are left at the edge: they treat you as a burden or an outsider, not as one of their own.
- Pre-language: Let into the huddle after a failed hunt as readily as after a good one, so long as she still pulls with the band; the one who shirks, cheats or feeds on the others is edged out, however able.

Face in the hierarchical deck: Included while you keep your place. The others keep you among them so long as you keep to what your place in the order calls for, high or low, and do not step out of line. The acceptance is real but conditional on keeping the place, not on the place itself (Status). You read it from how they treat you while you keep it and from the standing threat of being dropped.
- 10: You have kept your place and the others keep you among them: at every gathering a place is made for you, whether high or low, as a matter of course.
- 1: You have stepped out of line and been dropped, or were never given a place at all: gatherings form without you and you are kept at the edge.
- Pre-language: Kept in the group, at whatever rank she holds, for as long as she does what her place calls for; the moment she oversteps, the others turn on her or shut her out, and she feels the edge of that long before it happens.

### Prestige: Prestige

Status: Tightened by the literature 3 Nov 2026 (docs/model-edits/tighten/); provisional

Continuum: HCP. Axis: Respect

How far you feel the others defer to you freely for what you bring the group: skill, knowledge or contribution it values. They watch how you do it, copy you, bring you problems, take your advice, hand you decisions and jobs that matter and take your word on them. The deference is given, not owed to any place in a rank order (Status). Being handed a decision or a job because of what you bring is part of it; whether the others think you will carry your share of the work is not. It fades once what you bring does, unless it has settled into your place (Status).

- 10: Whenever your kind of skill or contribution counts, the others turn to you, watch and copy how you do it, bring you their problems and take your advice.
- 1: No one asks you, watches you or copies you; when your kind of skill or contribution is needed they turn to someone else.
- Pre-language: Being the one others watch, copy and seek out: eyes on her hands, bodies drawn near while she works, problems brought to her, her way of doing things taken up, the giver sought as a partner; as children and apes attend to and copy skilled or much-attended models, and as the most giving members of a group are sought out and honoured. Intimates read it from who gets watched, copied and sought, and who is passed over when a task of her kind comes up.

Effect on Engagement: Where you feel that others have a high opinion of your competence and trustworthiness: you feel valued and understood; you feel that others regard you to be a legitimate and useful member of the group; your identity as belonging to this group is consistent with others' appraisal of you.

## Force cards

Increment lines describe the target rising, decrement lines the target falling. Reinforce: increment follows a high source. Inverse: increment follows a low source. Discretionary: the direction is set by a named circumstance.

### E:Belonging->DivsExp: Making Fetch Happen (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Revised 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars (reword); provisional. Origin: V1 card Popularity->DivsExp (Making Fetch Happen), renamed to the draft Pillars; reread verdict: keep.

- Increment: When the others draw me in as one of their own, and after a knock one of them comes and sits with me, my bracing eases; I expect a slip, a mood or a disagreement of mine to be taken as coming from one of their own, and my felt safety to show myself rises.
- Decrement: When gatherings form without me and no one comes to me after a knock, I expect a slip or a disagreement of mine to be met as an outsider's would be, with a rebuff, and I mask myself and brace: my felt safety to show myself falls.
- Explanation: Consolation by a valued partner lowers stress behaviour in chimpanzees after aggression (Fraser, Stahl and Aureli 2008), and it comes mostly from partners with valuable relationships, which is the felt core of being drawn in. A member who is wanted expects her exposure to be borne as one of their own; one left at the edge expects it to be held against her. Felt safety in teams is a shared reading that interpersonal risk is acceptable (Edmondson 1999).
- Pre-language (increment): When others seek her out and play with her, she lets her moods and fumbles show without watching faces.
- Pre-language (decrement): When others leave her at the edge, she watches faces, masks her reactions and braces for a snap.

### E:Belonging->Levelling: Friends at my back (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Generated for the draft model; provisional, awaiting Ben Rechecked 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars: kept.. Origin: Generated 31 Oct 2026 by a clean evolutionary anthropologist session, blind to V1 cards; chosen by blind review. Sources are the generator's own and have not been independently checked.

- Increment: When I feel the others want me among them, I sense they would close round me if anyone tried to push me about, and my sense that no one can lord it over me rises.
- Decrement: When gatherings form without me and no one seeks me out, I feel I would stand alone if someone pushed me about, and my sense that no one can lord it over me falls.
- Explanation: Felt protection from domination rests on expected coalitional support, and being bonded into the group is the felt basis of that expectation; an isolated member is exposed.
- Pre-language (increment): When others seek her out and groom her, she faces a swaggering member without shrinking, expecting her companions to come to her side, and her sense of no bully rises.
- Pre-language (decrement): When others leave her on her own, she shrinks when a forceful member nears, with no one to run to, and her sense of no bully sinks.

### E:Belonging->Reciprocity: One of us, so it comes back (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Revised 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars (replace); provisional. Origin: Generated 31 Oct 2026 by a clean evolutionary anthropologist session, blind to V1 cards; chosen by blind review. Sources are the generator's own and have not been independently checked.

- Increment: When the others draw me in as one of their own, I feel sure that what I put in will come back from the group and that what I need will be met.
- Decrement: When they leave me at the edge and treat me as a burden, I feel that what I give goes into a hole and that my needs would be passed over.
- Explanation: In the return-from-the-group face, the group gives to those it counts as its own and passes over those it does not. People expect generalised returns from their own group and not from outsiders (bounded generalised reciprocity: Yamagishi and Kiyonari 2000), so being wanted as one of them is the felt sign that her own need will be met, and being edged out is the sign that it will be passed over.
- Pre-language (increment): When others seek her out and draw her in, she reaches for a share of what they have without hesitation, expecting it to be handed over, and her sense that giving comes back grows.
- Pre-language (decrement): When others leave her at the edge, she hangs back from the food and holds what she has close, expecting nothing to come her way, and her sense that giving comes back sinks.

### E:DivsExp->Belonging: Open or masked (Discretionary, Egalitarian)

Status: Fixed 3 Nov 2026 after the final high and low check (second round); provisional. Origin: V1 card DivsExp->Popularity (None), reworded by a clean reread for the draft Pillar texts (reword): The circumstance (how my contribution lands, and whether I am already wanted) still sets the direction, but the explanation names popularity and its pre-language names being liked.

- Explanation: Whether feeling safe to show myself draws the others closer depends on what I would be showing them. Circumstance one: I pull my share of the work, so what shows is moods, owned slips and disagreements aired for the group's sake. HIGH: I let them show, the others read me as one of their own and draw me in, so my Belonging rises. LOW: braced and masked, I come across as aloof even while I pull my share, and they leave me at the edge of the talk, so my Belonging falls. Circumstance two: what I have to show is mostly a run of needs and complaints the others would have to carry, and they have begun to tire of carrying them. HIGH: I go on letting them show, I read more and more as a burden and they edge away, so my Belonging falls. LOW: braced and masked, I keep those needs to myself; with nothing new to carry, they stop edging away and let me back into the talk, so my Belonging rises. Someone already wanted among them is borne with longer. Masked expression lowers a partner's rapport and wish to affiliate (Butler et al. 2003), and disclosure draws liking (Collins and Miller 1994), for circumstance one.
- Pre-language (explanation): When she does her share alongside the others, showing her moods and fumbles freely draws them to her, while going still and masked leaves her on the edge. When the others have begun to turn from her constant wants and complaints, showing more of them makes them turn further away, while keeping them hidden lets them come back to her and make room for her again.

### E:DivsExp->GrpProsp: Mistakes in the open (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Revised 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars (replace); provisional. Origin: V1 card DivsExp->GrpProsp (Font of Wisdom), reworded by a clean reread for the draft Pillar texts (reword): Felt safety still feeds the group's prospects (safe members put their input forward), but the lines make the source my input being taken up or followed, an uptake reading the new text does not define.

- Increment: When I feel safe to let a slip or a disagreement show, I sense that troubles with the venture come out where the group can deal with them, and I feel surer it can pull the venture off.
- Decrement: When I brace and mask myself, I sense that slips and doubts are being hidden around me as well as by me, and I feel less sure the group can pull the venture off.
- Explanation: Psychological safety lets errors and disagreements surface early, which is how teams learn and correct course (Edmondson 1999). A member's own felt safety is her best read of whether that is happening, so it carries into her confidence in the group's capability.
- Pre-language (increment): When she is loose among them and her fumbles on the shared venture draw help rather than jeers, she sees fumbles mended as they happen and goes at the venture with confidence.
- Pre-language (decrement): When she is braced and hides her fumbles, she sees others hide theirs too, and the venture feels shakier to her.

### E:DivsExp->Levelling: Free to scoff, free of bullies (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Revised 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars (reword); provisional. Origin: Generated 31 Oct 2026 by a clean social psychologist session, blind to V1 cards; chosen by blind review. Sources are the generator's own and have not been independently checked.

- Increment: When I feel safe to let a disagreement show, I bristle or scoff at anyone who starts to push me about or help themselves to more than their due, and I feel no one here could make me do, drop or give up anything, or keep more than their due, without an argument from me: my sense of Levelling rises.
- Decrement: When I brace and mask myself, I let a pushy member's demands and grabs pass without a murmur and do what he wants, and I feel I am being made to comply: my sense of Levelling falls.
- Explanation: The everyday tools of levelling are ridicule, criticism and open objection (Boehm). A member who feels safe to show herself uses them on reflex against anyone who pushes her or grabs, and since others tend to join such sanctions, force and grabbing get less grip on her; one who is braced swallows her objection, gives way and feels the force.
- Pre-language (increment): When she is loose among the others, she bristles, mocks or protests when a swaggering member pushes at her, and goes about her business beside him without a glance at his mood.
- Pre-language (decrement): When she is braced, she goes still and gives way when a swaggering member pushes at her, and watches his mood.

### E:DivsExp->OutResp: No cover-ups (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Revised 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars (replace); provisional. Origin: V1 card DivsExp->OutResp (Rise to the Occasion), reworded by a clean reread for the draft Pillar texts (reword): The mechanism holds, but both lines turn on my ideas being heard or going unheard (the old being-listened-to reading) rather than my felt safety to put them forward.

- Increment: When I feel safe to let my own slips show, I take it that no one here needs to dress up a report, so when a member says a part is done I take it as done and leave it in their hands: my confidence that they will do their part rises.
- Decrement: When I brace and mask my own slips, I take it that the others dress up their reports as I would, so 'done' no longer means done and I check what they say they have finished: my confidence that they will do their part falls.
- Explanation: Where showing a slip draws a put-down, members cover their shortfalls, and whether problems are raised depends on felt safety (Edmondson 1999), so a report that work is done cannot be taken at face value. Caution: units with more openness report more errors (Edmondson 1996), so in a safe group she also hears of more slips. The card holds because what moves OutResp here is whether reports can be trusted, not how many slips she hears of; a run of owned and fixed slips reads as reliability, a run of unfixed ones belongs on an OutResp evidence card. The step from her own safety to her reading of others' reports is a proposal; keep it modest.
- Pre-language (increment): When she is loose among them and shows her own fumbles, she leaves a shared task in another's hands and turns away without looking back.
- Pre-language (decrement): When she is braced and hides her own fumbles, she keeps glancing back at a shared task left with another, and goes over it herself.

### E:DivsExp->Reciprocity: Need shown, need met (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: Revised 3 Nov 2026 against the tightened Pillars (replace); provisional. Origin: V1 card DivsExp->IndRecip (Growth Mindset), reworded by a clean reread for the draft Pillar texts (reword): The mechanism holds, but the increment makes the source my ideas actually being heard, and the pre-language makes it others following, rather than my felt safety to voice them.

- Increment: When I feel safe to let a need show, I let it show, and seeing it met I feel surer that what I put into this group comes back.
- Decrement: When I brace and keep even a plain need masked, it goes unmet, and I feel that what I give here does not come back.

### X:DivsExp->Prestige: Ideas aired, ideas taken up (Reinforce, X)

Status: provisional. Origin: Proposed by the social psychologist's critique of VX (5 Nov 2026)

- Increment: When I feel safe to let a disagreement or a half-formed idea show, I put forward how we might do the work better in front of the others; they take it up, bring me the next problem of that kind and look to me when it comes round again: my sense that they turn to me for what I bring rises.
- Decrement: When I brace and keep my ideas masked, what I could bring stays in my head; the others never see it, take the next problem to whoever spoke up, and I watch my kind of skill go to them: my sense that they turn to me for what I bring falls.
- Explanation: Speaking up raises the status colleagues confer, partly because those who speak are seen as more agentic and competent (Weiss and Morrison 2019). Promotive voice, ideas for doing things better, raised peer-rated status and later leader emergence, while prohibitive voice, raising concerns, did not; men gained more than women (McClean et al. 2018). Members who act confidently are judged competent and gain influence even when their answers are no better (Anderson and Kilduff 2009). The card covers ideas offered to improve the shared work; voicing alarm or criticism is not covered and may cost her. Set it smaller for women members. Deck X: DivsExp is egalitarian, Prestige hierarchical. It forms a Reinforce loop with X:Prestige->DivsExp; the brake is a visible failure of what she puts forward (Prestige evidence).

### E:OutResp->Levelling: No passengers (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: provisional. Origin: Proposed by the social psychologist's critique of VX (5 Nov 2026)

- Increment: When every member carries through what they take on, no one is eating from a pot they did not fill, and each share matches what each has put in: my sense that no one here takes more than their due and keeps it rises.
- Decrement: When some members coast and still take the same share of credit and reward as those who carry them, I see them taking what they have not earned and getting away with it, and I feel a sucker for carrying them: my sense that no one here takes more than their due and keeps it falls.
- Explanation: Members cut their own effort rather than be the sucker who carries a capable partner who free rides, a reaction to broken equity and reciprocity norms (Kerr 1983). Negative emotion towards free riders drives costly punishment of them (Fehr and Gächter 2002). A free rider who shares the group's take gets more than the group's reckoning gives, which is what Levelling's second limb reads. The card holds where the take is shared; where reward tracks each member's own input, shirking costs the shirker and Levelling is untouched. Modest; forms a Reinforce loop with E:Levelling->OutResp.

### X:Prestige->DivsExp: Credit to spend (Reinforce, X)

Status: provisional. Origin: Proposed by the social psychologist's critique of VX (5 Nov 2026)

- Increment: When the others turn to me for what I bring and take my advice, I feel a slip of mine would be put down to a bad day rather than held against me, and I let my doubts and disagreements show: my felt safety to show myself rises.
- Decrement: When no one turns to me any more, I feel every slip would be counted against me with nothing to set against it, so I keep my doubts and disagreements masked: my felt safety to show myself falls.
- Explanation: Higher status goes with greater felt psychological safety (Nembhard and Edmondson 2006); standing releases approach and expression, while low standing primes attention to threat and inhibition (Keltner, Gruenfeld and Anderson 2003). Hollander's idiosyncrasy credit says members who have earned standing are allowed more latitude, but direct tests are few and weak, so the card rests on the safety and power evidence. X:Status->DivsExp carries the route through settled place; this card carries live deference and should be the weaker of the two.

### E:GrpProsp->Reciprocity: Good season, open hands (Reinforce, Egalitarian)

Status: provisional. Origin: Proposed by the social psychologist's critique of VX (5 Nov 2026)

- Increment: When the venture looks well within our reach, I am buoyed, I help whoever asks without counting, and the others do the same around me: my reading that help here comes back rises.
- Decrement: When the venture looks beyond us, everyone hunches over their own part, help asked for is slow to come and mine goes unreturned: my reading that help here comes back falls.
- Explanation: Good mood raises helping (Carlson, Charlin and Miller 1988). Mood spreads through a group by contagion, and positive contagion brings more cooperation and less conflict, negative contagion the reverse (Barsade 2002). Confidence in the venture carries that mood, so help flows more freely in a group that expects to win. Under threat, attention narrows (Staw et al. 1981), and each member guards her own part. The step from the group's mood to her felt return is a proposal: common sense, since a winning side is a generous one. Modest; forms a loop with E:Reciprocity->GrpProsp.
